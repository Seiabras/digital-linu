      FAQ

      How much does it cost?

      Each service shows its “from” price, and the package adds up as you go: what is a one-off delivery (a logo, a company video) and what is monthly (management, ads). The exact price comes in the proposal, in writing, with no commitment.

      Is the ad spend included?

      No. The money that goes to Meta or Google is yours and is paid straight to them, on your card. The service covers setting up, monitoring and adjusting the campaigns.

      Do I have to take everything?

      No. You can ask for a single service. The packages are suggestions, and you change whatever you like.

      Is there a minimum contract?

      Only a 3-month minimum for ads and automation, which need setting up at the start. After that, and for the other monthly services from day one, it is month to month: to stop, just give 30 days’ notice, with no penalty.

      How does payment work?

      One-off delivery: half when you accept the proposal and half on delivery. Monthly: at the start of each month. You get an invoice in euros and pay by SEPA transfer through Wise. Between businesses, where it applies, VAT is under the reverse charge: the buyer accounts for it in their own country.

      How many revisions are included?

      Two rounds of changes on each delivery (a video, a logo, the month’s designs). Fixing our own mistakes does not count.

      Do I film the videos myself?

      Yes: you film on your phone, with a script and tips from the team, and get the edited video back. On-site filming by the team is available in Brazil only.

      Do you work with clients outside Brazil?

      Yes. Everything is done remotely, in English or Portuguese, with calls arranged for your time zone. We are based in Brazil (UTC−3), and replies come within one business day.
